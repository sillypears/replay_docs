Validated on **35 files**: 33 full, 2 partial (online matches interleave extra cosmetic records). Decoder: `src/extract_metadata.py:parse_tlv_header()`.

## Layout

```text
m = d[0x10:0x58]  (72B)

[02][stage u32][00000000]              stage (see Stage IDs)
[01][02][ff000000]                      constant template
[00][02][ff000000]                      constant template
[00][00][p1_char u32][p1_skin u32]      Player 1  (11B: slot, pad, char, skin)
[01][p1_extra1][p1_extra2]
[04][00000000]                          marker (5B; online: may carry 1B pads)
[01][p2_char][p2_skin]                  Player 2
[01][p2_extra1][p2_extra2]
ffffffff                                terminator (absent in instant-quit)
```

- Player blocks found via regex `00 00 (char)(skin)` and `\x04\x00\x00\x00\x01(char)(skin)` — validated rather than fixed-offset walking (instant-quit recordings shift by one byte).
- `stage` from first record `[02]` — the only stage-bearing record.
- Skins `255` = "none" sentinel; real skins `0..300` (defaults: Lox 126, Cla 17, Gou 89, Kra 97, Lar 114, Absa 4, Zetterburn 232, Orcane 171, …).
